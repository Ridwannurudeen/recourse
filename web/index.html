Creditcoin CC3 testnet · Evidence route

Verified conduct.
Enforceable credit.

Recourse uses Attestcoin proofs of Ethereum conduct to enforce undercollateralised credit on Creditcoin. A proven breach freezes undrawn credit, applies a penalty bond against debt, and pays the hunter who submitted the proof.

The proof does not release an escrow. It changes credit risk.

The documented V1 operator run detected, proved and submitted an outflow without a human step. It monitored Uniswap v4’s shared PoolManager, an unrelated public third-party contract chosen for continuous activity. This demonstrates prospective adjudication mechanics, not a borrower covenant violation.

Beyond the V1 demonstrations

Deployed coordination.
Published interfaces.

The policy kernel, proof-job market, operator market and portfolio pool are deployed on CC3 testnet. V3 has an activated test-token facility and funded proof job; it has not yet adjudicated a proof, and V3 operator execution is not enabled. The operator market is empty. The portfolio allocation used project wallets and test tokens.

recourse-protocol-sdk@0.1.1 is published for interface discovery and testnet integration. Interfaces are not frozen; no external integration is demonstrated.

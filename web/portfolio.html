Truth boundary

What these totals mean

  • Facility limit, funded principal, debt, bond, and available credit are contract state—not market value, recoverable value, or expected return.
  • Token symbols and decimals are issuer-declared metadata. Asset identity is the chain ID plus contract address; symbol text never determines grouping.
  • No capital-pool share price, utilization yield, loss rate, or liquidity claim is inferred from this inventory.
  • Coverage reports exactly how many factory entries were read. Partial reads remain labeled and are not extrapolated.
  • This is a single-endpoint RPC assertion, not independent consensus verification. It is not suitable as the sole input for value-moving decisions.

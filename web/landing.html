Enforceable credit.

Recourse turns cryptographic evidence into enforceable credit covenants on Creditcoin. A proven breach freezes undrawn credit, applies a penalty bond against debt, and pays the hunter who submitted the proof.

The documented V1 operator run detected, proved and submitted an outflow without a human step. It monitored Uniswap v4’s shared PoolManager, an unrelated public third-party contract chosen for continuous activity. This demonstrates prospective adjudication mechanics, not a borrower covenant violation.

What this state does—and does not—prove

Accepted observations prove included, successful source transactions and their encoded receipt logs. They are event deltas or transitions, not current balances, storage snapshots, prices, or source-chain timestamps.

Proof time is CC3 acceptance time. The displayed rUSD is a fixed-supply testnet demonstration asset, not a production stablecoin. No external audit or pilot is claimed.

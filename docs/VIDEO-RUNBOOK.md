# Recourse — demo video runbook (target 3:10, hard cap 4:00)

Record at 1080p, one continuous screen capture with voice-over. Every claim is backed by something on
screen; say nothing the screen does not show. Read every address and figure from the page or the
explorer, never from memory.

**Disclosure discipline — non-negotiable.** Both recorded adjudications ran on the **V1** generation
against **unrelated third-party addresses**. Say so out loud. Never call either one a "catch" of the
borrower. Say "V3 has not adjudicated a proof" once. Never say audited, production, customer, or
partner.

## 0:00-0:35 — Open on the evidence page

Open `https://recourse.gudman.xyz/`. Let the first screen sit for a beat, then scroll slowly to the
metric band.

> "Recourse is undercollateralised credit on Creditcoin, where cryptographic proofs of Ethereum
> conduct enforce the covenant. A proven breach freezes undrawn credit, applies the borrower's bond
> against debt, and pays whoever submitted the evidence. The proof does not release an escrow — it
> changes credit risk. Everything on this page is checkable, and the check is two commands."

## 0:35-1:05 — The command, run live

Click the command block to copy. Cut to a terminal, paste, run. Let the output land.

> "No wallet, no Foundry, no environment file. It re-checks every on-chain claim this project makes
> against public endpoints: forty-six pass, none fail. Three come back UNVERIFIED — the local test
> counts, authorship, and whether the operator really ran unattended. A public RPC cannot establish
> those, so the checker refuses to claim them."

**If the live run is slow, pre-record this segment and cut to the finished output.** Do not sit in
silence for forty seconds.

Scroll to `FIG. 1` and receipt 01.

> "The first record is a prospective adjudication. The covenant was committed on Creditcoin two
